Household Electricity Consumption

Next-hour demand forecast

Enter recent consumption and the target hour. A Random Forest trained on lag and calendar features, running entirely in this page, predicts what the household will draw next.

Consumption is the household's average active power draw (kW) over the hour.
Weekend flag: 0 (weekday)
Enter the four consumption values and hit forecast, the prediction and its uncertainty range will appear here.
Runs a 200-tree Random Forest (scikit-learn, exported to JSON) directly in browser, no server, no data sent anywhere. Peak threshold shown on the gauge is the 90th percentile of this project's training data (3.23 kW). This is a demonstration of the model built for a UTS Machine Learning coursework project; forecasts are illustrative, not a real household energy readout.
